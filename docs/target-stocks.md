For the **wheel strategy**, the sweet spot is usually:

* Stocks you would happily own for 6–24 months
* Strong options liquidity (tight bid/ask spreads)
* Reasonably elevated implied volatility (higher option premiums)
* Meaningful dividends
* Large-cap, financially stable businesses

You generally want to avoid chasing the absolute highest IV names because many have high IV for a reason (earnings risk, weak balance sheets, biotech binary events, etc.). [\[options.cafe\]](https://options.cafe/blog/best-stocks-for-wheel-strategy/)

## Personal Screening Rules I Would Use

To build a watchlist, I typically look for:

* Dividend yield > 2.5%
* Average stock volume > 2 million shares/day
* Average options volume > 1,000 contracts/day
* IV Rank roughly 20–60%
* Market cap > $10B
* Positive free cash flow
* No imminent earnings announcement
* Comfortable holding through a 20–30% drawdown

Those criteria align with common wheel-selection guidance emphasizing liquidity, reasonable IV, and quality underlying businesses. [\[options.cafe\]](https://options.cafe/blog/best-stocks-for-wheel-strategy/)
